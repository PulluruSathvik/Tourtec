## 🚖 New Feature: Source-to-Destination Route Finder & Transport Booking

### 1. 🔍 Custom Place-to-Place Navigation
- **Input Any Source & Destination**: Type or select any starting point (Hotel, Railway Station, Airport, Ghat, Temple) and ending point.
- **⇄ 1-Click Swap**: Instantly reverse direction.
- **Popular Trip Presets**:
  - 🚆 *Varanasi Cantt Station ➔ Kashi Vishwanath Temple*

### 3. 🎫 Direct 1-Click Booking Integration
Direct external deep-links connected to India's leading transport apps:
- 🚖 **Uber & Ola Cabs**: Instant ride booking with pre-filled dropoff coordinates.
- 🛵 **Rapido**: Instant Auto and Bike taxi booking.
- 🚌 **RedBus & State E-Bus**: City bus passes and tickets.
- 🛥️ **UP Tourism Smart City Solar Boat**: Direct boat booking link.
- 🗺️ **Google Maps Live GPS**: Instant turn-by-turn walking and driving GPS directions.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Open **`http://localhost:3000/`**.
2. Under the **Trip Plan & Map** tab, click the top button:
   ```text
   🚖 Place-to-Place Route & Ticket Booking
   ```
3. Enter any **Starting Point** and **Destination**.
4. View the compared transit options and click any **1-Click Booking Button** (e.g. *Book on Uber*, *Book on Rapido*, *Book UP Tourism Solar Boat*, or *Start Live GPS*).
